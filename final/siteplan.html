Website Planning Document

Emergencia Cell Repair

Site Name

Emergencia Cell Repair

This name represents my local business dedicated to smartphone repair, accessories, and technical support. It is short, easy to remember, and clearly communicates the services offered.

Site Purpose

The website will provide information about smartphone repair services, available accessories, pricing, and business contact information. Visitors will also be able to browse products, request repair services, and contact the business through an online form.

Scenarios

  • How much does it cost to replace my phone screen?
  • Which accessories are currently available?
  • How can I request a repair online?

Color Scheme

Primary Color (#0B5ED7)

Buttons, headings and links.

Secondary Color (#F4F7FA)

Backgrounds and content cards.

Dark Color (#1F2937)

Body text and footer.

Typography

Montserrat will be used for headings, body text, buttons and navigation to maintain a clean and modern design.
