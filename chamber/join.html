NP Membership

This no-cost membership level is available to qualifying nonprofit organizations.

Benefits

  • Access to Chamber networking events
  • Organization listing in the business directory
  • Community partnership opportunities
  • Access to educational workshops

Bronze Membership

A basic membership designed for entrepreneurs and small businesses.

Benefits

  • Business directory listing
  • Access to networking events
  • Member training opportunities
  • Chamber news and business resources

Silver Membership

A membership level offering increased exposure and professional development opportunities.

Benefits

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Priority registration for Chamber events
  • Additional business directory promotion
  • Discounts on selected events and workshops

Gold Membership

Our premium membership level provides maximum visibility and participation within the Chamber.

Benefits

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Featured placement in Chamber promotions
  • Exclusive invitations to leadership events
  • Premium sponsorship and advertising opportunities
  • Priority support from Chamber representatives
